AirControl 0.1.2

Update for Android 8.0+. Install it over your current AirControl (do not uninstall); same signing key. New: a live practice box that shows what the camera sees, steadier gesture holding, camera error handling, a power-saving idle mode, and an optional "Lock screen" action (Android 9+, off by default, locks only).

Not tested on a real phone yet: gestures, scroll/tap, pointer, direct call, lock screen, camera sharing, photo/video saving, battery and heat. Custom gestures are experimental. The camera stops when the screen turns off; tap Start in the app to resume. The app cannot see the screen, so it cannot tell what a tap will press.
