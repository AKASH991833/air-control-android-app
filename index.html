AirControl 0.1.1

Update for Android 8.0+. Install it over your current AirControl (do not uninstall); same signing key. New: animated hand demos for every gesture, a How it works guide, simpler settings with Advanced tuning hidden.

Not tested on a real phone yet: gestures, scroll/tap, pointer, direct call, camera sharing, photo/video saving. Custom gestures are experimental. The app cannot see the screen, so it cannot tell what a tap will press.
